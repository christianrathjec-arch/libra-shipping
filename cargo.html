Three families, one desk.

Libra fixes dry cargo: bulk, break-bulk and project cargo, in parcels from 500 to 50,000 tonnes on sea-going vessels. Three families make up most of the book: agri, steel, and minerals and fertilisers.

The list below is what we carry most. Every trade is different, so if your cargo is not on it, ask us anyway. Chances are we have fixed it before.

Agri
Grain products[barley][rapeseed (meal)][soyabean meal][…]

Grain, feed meals and seeds are sensitive to moisture and contamination. We fix them on vessels with a checked cargo history and holds that are inspected before loading, under the GMP+ feed-safety scheme. What GMP+ means →

Steel
[pipes][coils][scrap][…]

Coils and pipes are high-value and easily damaged by rust or poor securing. They need the right hold, proper dunnage and lashing. Scrap is rough cargo that asks a lot of the ship. We know which vessels and which crews handle each.

Minerals & fertilisers
MineralsFertilisers[…]

Many fertilisers absorb moisture and cake, and some fall under the IMSBC Code for bulk cargoes that need special care. We fix the vessel that meets the cargo rules and keep the paperwork right, so the parcel arrives as it was loaded.

Forms of cargo.

Bulk

Loose cargo, loaded by grab or conveyor and measured in tonnes. Grain, minerals, fertilisers, scrap.

Break-bulk

Separate units such as coils, bundles, pipes or big bags, each lifted and secured on its own.

Project cargo

Heavy or oversized one-off pieces that need engineered lifting, stowage and securing.

Part cargo or full ship.

Parcels run from 500 to 50,000 tonnes: from a small coaster to a handymax bulker. One desk handles both the small lot and the full ship.

Certifications and conditions.

GMP+ GMP+ certifiedFeed Safety Assurance · certificate [number] · valid until [date] What this meansClose

GMP+ is the international feed-safety assurance scheme for the animal-feed chain. Everyone who handles feed materials between the field and the feed mill works under audited rules, and that includes the chartering and sea transport of grain, feed meals and seeds.

For a feed cargo it comes down to four things. The vessel’s last three cargoes are documented, and certain previous cargoes rule her out. The loading compartments are inspected before loading: empty, clean, dry and free of odour. Cleaning follows a set regime. And the paperwork travels with the cargo. The scheme gives the chartering party an essential role in that exchange of information between cargo owner and ship, which is exactly where Libra sits.

So a feed cargo fixed through Libra Shipping moves under procedures that an independent certification body checks, not only we ourselves.
