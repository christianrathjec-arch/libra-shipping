What chartering means for you.

You have cargo to move. We find the vessel that fits it, negotiate the freight and the terms with the owner, draw up the charter party and follow the voyage through to discharge. One broker, one line, from the first enquiry to the last tonne over the rail.

We have in-house expertise in the European short sea markets of the North Sea, the Atlantic, the Mediterranean and the Baltic, and we are equally well placed to cover your sea transport requirements worldwide. As the in-house chartering broker of Euro Nordic, we fix the most effective solution whether you need a vessel for 500 or 50,000 metric tonnes. And with every fixture, you move your cargo by the most environmentally friendly modality there is.

The ship is our trade. The cargo does not stop at the rail.

Chartering is everything about hiring the ship. Forwarding is everything about moving the cargo through the chain, from the factory gate to the receiver’s yard. The two overlap on the sea leg, and that is exactly where Libra sits. Once we have fixed the vessel, we can take the rest of the chain off your hands as well.

  • Pre- and on-carriage by barge, rail or road
  • Transhipment and storage
  • Shipping documents, bills of lading
  • Customs and export or import formalities
  • Cargo handling, loading and discharge
  • Cargo insurance where needed
