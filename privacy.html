Privacy statement.

What Libra Shipping B.V. does with the personal data you give us, and what we do not do.

Who we are

Libra Shipping B.V., Waalhaven Oostzijde 85, 3087 BM Rotterdam, the Netherlands. Chamber of Commerce number 24126021. For anything about your data, write to chartering@librashipping.nl.

What we collect

Only what you give us. When you use the contact form, call us or email us, that is your name, company, email address, telephone number and whatever you tell us about your cargo, vessel or port call. We do not collect anything about you from other sources.

Why we use it

To answer your enquiry, to prepare a quotation and, if we work together, to carry out the chartering or agency service you have asked for. We also keep what we need to meet our legal and accounting obligations. We do not use your data for marketing and we never sell it.

Who else sees it

Only the parties needed to do the job: for example shipowners, charterers, terminals, port authorities, pilots and customs. They receive what is necessary for the shipment or the port call and nothing more. The companies that host our email and this website process data on our behalf under agreements that keep it confidential.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to business are deleted within a year. Records of services we have carried out are kept for seven years, as Dutch tax law requires.

This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics and does not track you. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, including your IP address, for a short period to keep the site secure. The fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to our use of it or ask for a copy. Email chartering@librashipping.nl and we will reply within a month. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

We will update this statement when our practices change. This version is dated October 2026.
