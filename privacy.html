In short
  • We only hold what you give us when you contact us about a cargo, vessel or port call.
  • We use it to answer you and to carry out the work. Never for marketing, never sold.
  • This website sets no cookies and does not track you.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

What we collect

Only what you give us. When you use the contact form, call us or email us, that is your name, company, email address, telephone number and whatever you tell us about your cargo, vessel or port call. If we work together, we also hold the details needed to carry out and invoice the service. We do not collect data about you from other sources.

Why we use it

To answer your enquiry, to prepare a quotation and, if we work together, to carry out the chartering or agency service you have asked for. We also keep what we need to meet our legal, accounting and tax obligations. We do not use your data for marketing and we never sell it.

Legal basis

We process your data because it is necessary to respond to your request and to perform the contract with you, and because we have a legitimate interest in running our business and keeping proper records. Where the law requires us to keep records, we do so on that legal obligation.

Who else sees it

Only the parties needed to do the job: for example shipowners, charterers, terminals, port authorities, pilots and customs. They receive what is necessary for the shipment or the port call and nothing more. The companies that host our email and this website process data on our behalf under agreements that keep it confidential. We do not transfer your data outside the European Economic Area unless the shipment itself requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to business are deleted within one year. Records of services we have carried out are kept for seven years, as Dutch tax law requires.

How we protect it

Your data is stored on secured systems that only our staff can access, and only those who need it for their work.

This website and cookies

This website does not use cookies or analytics and does not track you. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, including your IP address, for a short period to keep the site secure. The fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to our use of it, restrict it or ask for a copy. Email chartering@librashipping.nl and we will reply within one month. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

We will update this statement when our practices change. The version and date are shown at the top of this page.
