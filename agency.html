Our agency workflow.

  1. 01AppointmentWe confirm your nomination the same day.
  2. 02Proforma DAA disbursement estimate before the port call, so there are no surprises.
  3. 03Pre-arrivalBerth, notices, terminal and services lined up prior to the vessel arriving.
  4. 04Vessel in portA dedicated operator monitoring from arrival until departure.
  5. 05DepartureClearance and sailing without delay.
  6. 06Final DA & documentsThe account settled, the related documents distributed and the final DA issued.
