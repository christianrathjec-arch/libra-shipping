Certifications and conditions.

GMP+ GMP+ certifiedFeed Safety Assurance · certificate [number] · valid until [date] What this meansClose

GMP+ is the international feed-safety assurance scheme for the animal-feed chain. It sets audited requirements for everyone who handles feed materials between the field and the feed mill, including the chartering, agency and transport of cargoes such as grain products, barley, rapeseed meal and soybean meal.

For a charterer or receiver it means one thing: a feed cargo fixed or handled through Libra Shipping moves under procedures that are checked by an independent certification body, not only by us. Vessel suitability, previous cargoes, cleaning and documentation are handled to the standard the scheme requires.
