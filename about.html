About · since 1976

Ship brokers and ship agents since 1976.

Libra Shipping can offer you the solution for your logistics, which includes chartering, agency, transhipment and storage.

Since 1976.

Libra Shipping was founded in Rotterdam in 1976 as a ship broker and ship agent for the coastal trade. Today it is a joint venture of Euro Nordic Logistics and Euro-Rijn, with a chartering desk in the Waalhaven and agencies in the Netherlands and Belgium.

The way of working has not changed much in fifty years: short lines, a person who knows both the vessel and the cargo, and a phone that is answered.

How we work.

Excellent service at competitive rates, with high-quality subcontractors behind us. That is the promise. Six things make it true, and they come in pairs.

  • Safe & compliant Safety first. Never traded away for speed or cost. Certified. Feed cargo under the GMP+ scheme, checked by an independent body.
  • Fast & available 24/7 manned office. Experienced operators on the line, day and night. Pro-active. A faster port turnaround, cost-efficient for owner and charterer.
  • Personal & tailored Dedicated operators. One operator per vessel type and cargo, who knows both. Tailor-made. Built around the call, not around a standard.

Backed by two groups.
